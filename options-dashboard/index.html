Aggregated Options Dashboard

A live options Time & Sale feed for every listed series across index, ETF, futures and all equities within SPY/QQQ, aggregated into premium and delta-adjusted notional flow for custom grouping.

  • C++17
  • Direct3D 11
  • Tastytrade dxLink
>600,000 seriesoptions series streamed live
1.25M topicssubscribed across 5 WebSocket connections
13.3M printsrecorded in one session and replayable tick for tick

What It Streams

Every option print carries the series' live Greeks.

GroupUnderlyingsSubscribed
FuturesES, MES, NQ, MNQ, YM, MYM, RTY, M2K, VXTime & Sale on all available contracts
Futures optionsES, MES, NQ, MNQ, RTYTime & Sale + Greeks per series
Index optionsSPX, NDX, DJX, RUT, VIXTime & Sale + Greeks per series
ETF optionsSPY, QQQ, DIA, IWMTime & Sale + Greeks per series
Equity optionsTop ~350 of the 518 equities within SPY/QQQTime & Sale + Greeks per series

*The equity list is rebuilt from the current S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 constituents at every launch, ordered by market cap.

Print Data:

  • Pure Time & Sale. Every print carries its aggressor side, print type and whether it was a spread leg, so a session can be reviewed later
  • Filtered. Prints with an UNDEFINED aggressor or unknown type are left out of the flow. CANCEL and CORRECTION prints reverse exactly what the original print added
  • Diagnostics. Running counts of CANCEL, CORRECTION, unknown-type and UNDEFINED-aggressor prints, plus feed lag between dxLink time and machine time at p50 and p99

How It Subscribes

The feed is built around the account's streaming limits, which I measured before building it.

Limits:

  • Capacity. 5 WebSocket connections × 10 channels × 25,000 topics per channel = 1.25M topics
  • Cost. Each options series takes 2 topics: Time & Sale and Greeks. Each underlying takes 2 more: Trade for an accurate spot price and Quote for bid/ask

Subscribing:

  • Split. Series are hashed across the connections, so each carries a near-equal share of both series and ticks
  • Pacing. Every channel has its own rate limiter. At about 850 topics per second in total, a full load takes over 20 minutes
  • Verification. A chain turns green only when every series in it has been accepted

Each Print:

  • Parse. Decoded with simdjson on the receiving connection's thread
  • Join. Tagged with the underlying's current price, the series' latest Greeks and, for futures options, the contract month it is written on
  • Publish. Sent to a shared-memory ring read by the Options Flow page, the tape windows and the ES/NQ chart, and archived to disk

The Options Flow Page

Each display aggregates the underlyings named in its title into three cumulative dollar curves on one time axis.

PaneCalculation
Net premium flowprice × contracts × multiplier
Net delta-adjusted notional flowspot × |delta| × contracts × multiplier
Net delta-adjusted notional held|net contracts held| × |current delta| × spot × multiplier, re-marked every 250 ms

*The first two value each print when it trades, from its Time & Sale and Greeks data. The third tracks net contracts held per series and multiplies by the current delta, which can give a more accurate read on moneyness as spot moves.

Sign Convention:

net = (call buys − call sells) − (put buys − put sells)

Buying calls and selling puts both count as bullish. Buy and sell come from each print's aggressor side.

Recording and Replay

Everything the screens depend on is recorded, so any session can be replayed through the same windows and the same analytics.

  • Recorded. Every print, underlying quote, Greeks change and per-second connection health. About 10 GB of total recorded history for a full-day session
  • Integrity. Each second is marked complete, late or incomplete, and the replay timeline is colored by it
  • Playback. 0.05× to 100× speed with scrubbing, keeping the recorded timing between prints

The Rest of the Program

Tape Windows:

Five live consoles: Futures, Futures Options, Index Options, ETF Options and Equity Options. Each option line shows price, size, aggressor side, spot, lag and the series' Greeks.

ES/NQ Chart:

Front-month ES and NQ prices at 60 fps, drawn from the same feed.
